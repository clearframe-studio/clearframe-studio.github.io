Upload Desk

Upload Desk is a private desktop tool used by one person to upload that person's own videos to their own video channels. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Privacy Policy

Upload Desk runs on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens and settings are stored only on that computer. We do not collect, share, sell or send personal data to any third party, and we do not operate a server that stores user data.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner also agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms of Service

Upload Desk is provided for the owner's personal use only, without any warranty. It is used only to upload and schedule the owner's own content.
